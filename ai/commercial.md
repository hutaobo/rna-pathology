# Commercial Use

AI-Driven Spatial Pathologist can be offered commercially in several ways.

## Recommended service ladder

### 1. Analysis service

The fastest path is to offer SPatho as an expert analysis service.

Typical delivery:

- customer provides Xenium outputs and project context
- workflows are run by the SPatho team
- customer receives reports, summaries, and artifact bundles

### 2. Managed commercial edition

The next step is a managed productized service.

Typical delivery:

- structured intake
- workflow execution
- customer-specific organ packs or templates
- report review and support

### 3. Enterprise private deployment

The highest-governance option is a private deployment.

Typical delivery:

- customer-managed infrastructure
- customer-managed OpenAI or local-model credentials
- private workflows and stricter governance

## Why a dual-track model makes sense

SPatho has a genuine inference cost when OpenAI-assisted analysis is enabled.
That means the product should not rely on a single free-for-all model.

The recommended pattern is:

- free or low-friction academic community access
- paid commercial use
- higher-touch enterprise options

This is close to the public positioning used by CyteType and similar AI analysis products.

## Commercial inquiry checklist

A typical first discussion should clarify:

- organ and disease area
- number of samples
- sensitivity of the data
- whether the customer wants managed inference or BYO key
- whether they need private deployment
