AI-Driven Spatial Pathologist

AI-Driven Spatial Pathologist is a product layer for spatial transcriptomics-driven pathology review. It combines cell type annotation, dendrogram-guided structure discovery, H&E-aware tissue interpretation, and AI-assisted report generation into a single workflow.

What the product does

Cluster Annotation

Builds evidence packs from differential expression and neighborhood context, then runs OpenAI-assisted or heuristic annotation with confidence, alternatives, and review priorities.

Structure Discovery

Propagates cluster annotations through dendrogram-guided tissue structure assignment, contour generation, clustermaps, and validation-ready spatial overlays.

Pathology Review

Produces case summaries, structure-level pathology interpretations, cluster reviews, HTML reports, and machine-readable artifact manifests.

Who it is for

Academic labs

Run local analyses on Xenium cases, compare tissue structures across samples, and generate review-ready reports for translational research.

Biotech and pharma teams

Use SPatho as a service or managed workflow for rapid pathology-focused interpretation of spatial transcriptomics studies.

Pathology and computational biology collaborators

Create a shared output contract across annotation, structure discovery, AI interpretation, and human review.

Edition model

The product is being positioned with two outward-facing tracks and one enterprise track:

Community Edition Commercial Team Edition Enterprise / Private Deployment

  • Community Edition: local package, non-commercial research use, bring-your-own OpenAI key or heuristic mode.
  • Commercial Team Edition: paid usage for customer projects, support, curated workflows, and managed delivery options.
  • Enterprise: private deployment, customer-managed credentials, and stricter governance for sensitive environments.

Current built-in organ packs

  • Lung: lung cancer Xenium workflows with lung-specific annotation taxonomy and review defaults.
  • Breast: breast cancer Xenium workflows with breast-specific annotation taxonomy and structure interpretation defaults.

More organ packs will follow as the public workflow contract stabilizes.
