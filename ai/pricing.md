# Pricing Direction

Pricing for AI-Driven Spatial Pathologist should reflect both software value
and the real cost of LLM-assisted analysis.

## Community Edition

Recommended positioning:

- non-commercial research use
- local installation
- bring-your-own OpenAI key or heuristic mode
- public documentation
- no formal SLA

This edition is best treated as a research-use entry point, not a managed service.

## Commercial Team Edition

Recommended positioning:

- paid use for customer and commercial projects
- support and onboarding
- report delivery and workflow assistance
- optional managed inference and managed runs

Recommended pricing model:

- annual platform or support fee
- usage-based fee per sample, batch, or project

## Enterprise

Recommended positioning:

- private deployment
- customer-managed credentials
- stricter security and governance options
- custom onboarding and support

Recommended pricing model:

- annual contract
- optional setup fee
- optional premium support tier

## Practical first step

For early customer work, the simplest pricing model is:

- one-off per-sample fee for pilot projects
- discounted project bundle for multi-sample work
- enterprise discussion for private deployments
